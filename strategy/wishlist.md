Open-ended, subjective, no lifecycle — per `AGENTS.md`'s `strategy/`
convention, this is a running list of directional ideas, not a tracked
project. An item graduates off this list once someone scopes it into a
real `projects/<name>/` entry.

- **Welcome emails to new PMC members and graduating projects.** Extends
  the graduation-outreach process already specified in
  [[2-year-strategic-plan]] (which welcomes newly-graduated TLPs) to also
  cover individual new PMC members, not just project-level graduations.
  Overlaps with [[ambassador-program]]'s Template A welcome email and its
  graduation-outreach rotation — check there before standing up a
  separate mechanism; this may just be "extend the existing template to
  a second audience" rather than a new build.
