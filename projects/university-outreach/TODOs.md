Pulled from `overview.md`'s "Open / unresolved" section — see that file
for full context on each.

- [ ] Liability posture for chapter events (physical meetups/hackathons
      under Apache branding) — trademark/endorsement optics are covered;
      actual legal exposure is not. Needs review from whoever handles
      this for the ASF, not improvised here.
- [ ] Chapter failure modes / off-ramps — no mechanism yet for ComDev to
      pull a chapter's registration/branding rights if it goes dormant,
      misrepresents affiliation, or produces low-quality activity.
- [ ] Naming/ownership of who actually builds the chapter-in-a-box
      materials — currently implicit ComDev ownership, no named volunteer
      or deadline.
- [ ] What to call the top-tier committer/PMC-adjacent recognition, if it
      needs a name at all — deliberately left open rather than inventing
      terminology.

Lower-priority, noted but not yet revisited: international considerations
(non-US IP regimes, export control, GDPR on an alumni/talent directory);
overlap-checking with existing corporate-sponsored community programs at
specific large projects before matching a chapter to them; CoC escalation
path at the local chapter/event level; good-first-issue quality
bootstrapping for PMCs without an existing curated newcomer backlog.

**Explicitly not on this list:** rollout/community-buy-in work — shelved
deliberately per the author, not an oversight (see overview.md's
"Explicitly out of scope").
