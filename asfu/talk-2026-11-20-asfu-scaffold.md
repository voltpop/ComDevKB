## Key points

- **Elevator pitch (Drew's words):** model government for tech kids, with a strong mentorship angle and a semi-professional society behind it.
- **Governance by analogy:** chapters mirror the foundation. A PMC-like committee comes first, and a board only when scale needs one. The committee decides real chapter business by lazy consensus.
- **Mentorship pipeline:** students (and staff) connect to project mentors for semi-guided entry into contributorship. The pipeline needs an outflow ("drain the pool"), so mentors are the real bottleneck, not campuses.
- **Mentorship shape:** not 1:1. Students pick projects by interest, language and availability.
- **Student incentive:** employment ("6 figure job territory"). Drew does not see motivation as a problem.
- **Culture first:** social above all. "Community Over Code" taken literally, like conversation over coffee. Drew wants to manufacture rituals and touchstones at the university level that flow up to the foundation. Drew's account is that the ASF currently has one ritual, the membership email, which is a template and could be copied down the ladder.
- **Branding and theme:** Nina proposed music as the advertising theme. Drew's refinement maps the subway-map idea (Rich implemented the map) to subway music, with projects as instruments in a mixed ensemble. Original naming was ASF@ or ALC@, where the @ links the ASF logo to the school logo. In Drew's words, @ implies location, not partnership, like an event ("Coca-Cola @ the beach").
- **The bigger opportunity:** Drew calls it a "problotunity" and wants it mapped. It is how ComDev curates the Apache Way. The discussion comes first: inventory what the culture is, then design.
- **Upkeep:** each chapter owes a monthly short report and proof of a meeting, plus a paced content slate (consumed, not binged). A quiet chapter gets a friendly Roll Call from a Shepherd, lifting ASF governance as close to 1:1 as it fits.
- **Pilots:** VSU, UNCC and HU are interested (UNCC and VSU are eager). VT is a planned approach.
- **Infrastructure needs:** a students.apache.org site and an ASF-wide Discord open to university people. Drew works on Infra and reports having the moderation covered and the ASF President's OK.
- **Ambassador link:** the Ambassador program is folded together with ASFU mentor recruiting. A role is a written "hole" with explicit requirements, and self-selecting volunteers fill it.

## Corrections

- **Mentoring:** project-level, not 1:1 (earlier suggestion was a per-mentor student cap).
- **Student incentive:** employment, not the BadgeFed credential. Retire that as the lead pitch.
- **CoC and liability:** lower priority than first stated.
- **Name overlap with ALC chapters:** resolved by ALCU naming.
- **Partnership guardrail:** it is a design choice, not a legal bar. A 501(c)(3) can partner. The earlier design avoided it for MOU delay and exclusivity reasons.
- **Shepherd and Roll Call:** a Shepherd is a Director assigned at random per report, and the Board (via a Director) asks for the Roll Call, which needs three responsive PMC members or risks the Attic. Not a standing per-project role.
- **Rollout:** [[overview]] says it is shelved. Pilot campuses are now interested and active.
