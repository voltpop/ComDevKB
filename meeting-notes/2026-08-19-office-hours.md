# ComDev office hours — 2026-08-19

## Topic: Ambassador program

**Raised by:** Ruth (ASF President, ComDev PMC member).

### Background

No prior thread in the KB before today — a new project, not an
extension of an existing one. A first draft already exists:
[proposed/ambassadors.md](https://github.com/apache/comdev-working-groups/blob/main/proposed/ambassadors.md),
written by Rich, a ComDev community member. Full detail lives in
[[ambassador-program]] (`projects/ambassador-program/overview.md`) — this
note is the meeting-facing summary, not a duplicate of it.

### What the draft proposes

**RECRUIT, EQUIP, CONNECT, RECOGNIZE** — ComDev never assigns ambassadors
from outside; each hosting PMC identifies its own community champion, and
ambassador responsibilities run to their own PMC, not to ComDev. The
weekly office hour is explicitly framed as a peer network, not a
reporting structure. Two-phase rollout: Phase 1 pilots outreach to five
named TLPs (Cassandra, Airflow, Iceberg, Kafka, Cloudstack); Phase 2
expands to the Top 20 by activity plus a standing graduation-outreach
process.

**Who gets selected, per Rich:** visible community leads —
people who already show up to events and do the work — not a formal
appointment process.

### Evaluation highlights (done ahead of this meeting)

- Matches how ComDev already operates: same tools/process/advice-not-
  authority pattern as GSoC, TAC, and the campus program; rotation design
  matches this KB's preference for distributed backstop authority over a
  single officer.
- Flagged overlaps to resolve, not blockers: the draft's "Speaker Travel
  Program" risked duplicating the existing Travel Assistance Committee;
  its Good First Issues aggregator risked duplicating the university
  outreach project's planned build; its peer-network venue and the
  separate community-forum-platform project may eventually want to be
  the same thing rather than two competing venues.
- Metrics dashboard is real, not aspirational — Rich is
  already publishing weekly ComDev metrics at
  <https://boxofclue.com/comdev-metrics/>, externally hosted, with a
  request to move it onto a ComDev-owned machine.

### Proposed refinement: hybrid funding for the Speaker Travel Program

Not yet raised with Rich or the PMC before today. Resolves
the TAC-duplication concern: donors fund a pool earmarked for speaker
travel; selection stays volunteer-led (ambassadors plus TAC-style
merit/geography criteria), never donor-driven — same vendor-neutrality
guardrail the university outreach project already established for
corporate sponsorship. Runs as its own effort, separate from TAC, given
its narrower audience (accepted-CFP speakers) and distinct funding
source.

### Prior art to check

Ruth suggested looking at the old Fedora Project/Red Hat
Ambassador program as precedent:
<https://fedoraproject.org/wiki/Ambassadors>. Not yet reviewed — raised
today, worth reading before/during this meeting to see what carries over
and what doesn't apply to ComDev's cross-project (rather than
single-distro) structure.
